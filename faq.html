FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

There's some questions I sometimes get, that I want to answer! So here are some frequently asked questions, with their answers.


Q: Does the game cost money?

A: Nope! Not at all! The game is 100% free-to-play with NO IAP! Although you can donate to support the game, but it's entirely up to your decision.


Q: How big is the game and how much RAM do I need to run it?

A: Currently as of v0.5.0, the game is ~200MB uncompressed, and requires at least 2 GB of RAM to run.


Q: What platforms does the game support?

A: Currently, the game only supports Windows. In the future, support for Linux will be added!


Q: Will you make a port for OSX/mobile/Chromebook/etc?

A: No, please don't ask for these. There's a slim chance it'll happen


Q: Will you add multiplayer?

A: Because of the current nature of the engine (EENGINE), adding multiplayer is heavily unlikely. Be it versus modes or co-op.


Q: Will there be official support for mods?

Yes! This is actually planned to happen after release! Expect modding capabilities baked into the game in a post-1.0 update.


Q: Can you add [insert character here] into the game?

A: Well there's an ongoing character contest in the TDRP Studios Discord Server! Though there are a few guidelines to follow, so make sure to read them.


Q: What genre is ESCAPE LIMTEX LABORATORIES?

A: It's a platformer and shooter.


Q: Can you configure keybinds?

A: As of v0.5.0, no, configurable keybinds is not supported.


Q: What is the intended audience for ESCAPE LIMTEX LABORATORIES?

A: ESCAPE LIMTEX LABORATORIES is intended for a 13+ audience, as it contains moderate bloodshed and violence.


Q: Will you make merch/plushies of characters from ESCAPE LIMTEX LABORATORIES?

A: ...you're getting a bit ahead of yourself, pal. The game ain't even fully out yet! For now, just get DELTARUNE's stuff, eh?


That's it for the FAQ! More is planned, see you soon!
